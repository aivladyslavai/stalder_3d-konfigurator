# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Private homeowners, architects, and property developers in Switzerland who are evaluating a premium pool and need to understand material, size, equipment, visual appearance, and indicative cost before requesting a consultation.

## Product Purpose

The configurator turns a complex pool purchase into one calm, visual decision surface. Users should be able to configure a STALDER pool without a multi-step wizard, see every change immediately in 3D, understand the price, and request an offer with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Swiss, precise, premium. The experience should feel composed and technically credible rather than decorative or sales-heavy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with excessive cards, pills, gradients, and soft shadows
- Toy-like configurators with bright colors, oversized rounding, and weak hierarchy
- Dense engineering software that exposes implementation complexity
- A literal visual copy of Berndorf; Berndorf is the usability reference, while STALDER should feel more refined

## Design Principles

1. Let the pool remain the hero; controls frame the 3D scene instead of competing with it.
2. Make expensive decisions feel calm through strong hierarchy, restrained color, and precise spacing.
3. Keep all configuration available on one screen while revealing detail progressively.
4. Make selection state unmistakable without relying on decoration.
5. Use Berndorf's directness and STALDER's premium Swiss character.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All controls need visible focus states, keyboard operation, sufficient contrast, non-color selection cues, and reduced-motion behavior.
